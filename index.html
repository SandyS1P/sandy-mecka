02 · Growth

One playbook, any group.

Grow by community, with quality and diversity built into every step.

  1. Start where customer demand meets easy access. First group: students. They’re phone-native, have the most free time, and campuses are dense networks, so referrals beat ads.

  2. Spread across programs, homes and jobs. Their mix sets the mix of everyone they bring.

  3. In-person kickoff, then 1:1 feedback until their first 3 clips pass.

  4. Higher pay, first pick of tasks, a Founding Contributor title, and a reference for their résumé.

  5. Both get paid after the newcomer’s first approved clip. Bigger bonus for people who fill a gap.

  6. Top contributors open the next campus or program.
